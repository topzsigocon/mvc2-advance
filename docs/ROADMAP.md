# Development roadmap

## Phase 1 — playable foundation
- Butano boot and frame loop
- Combat state model and two-fighter test round
- Input mapping, health, movement, hitstun, guard and attacks
- Next: create original sprite/tile assets and implement the rendering/HUD layer

## Phase 2 — team combat
- Three-character team data and team order selection
- Tag switching and character-specific assists
- CPU team logic

## Phase 3 — advanced combat
- Special move command parser
- Hyper meter, level 1–3 hypers and team hyper
- Super jump, air dash, chain and air combos

## Phase 4 — content
- Original roster and portraits
- Stages and modes
- Boss/ending progression

## Phase 5 — polish
- Original music and sound effects
- Save records/unlocks
- Hardware profiling and emulator/real-hardware QA
